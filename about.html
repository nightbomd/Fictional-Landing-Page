Our Family & History

Rooted in Three Generations

A legacy built on hard work, fertile soil, and family tradition.

For over fifty years, the Hollow Creek family has poured its heart into cultivating this land. What started as a modest greenhouse operation has grown into a thriving three-generation family farm. Today, grandparents, children, and grandchildren work side-by-side to preserve our heritage while introducing sustainable agricultural practices for the future.

We believe that the best food is grown with care, harvested by hand, and shared with the people you love. When you visit us, you aren't just buying local produce—you are becoming a part of our extended family story.

Fresh From Our Fields To Your Table

Pure, nutrient-dense fruits and vegetables picked daily.

Our pride and joy lies in the quality of what we grow. From crisp heirloom tomatoes and sweet summer corn to crisp autumn apples, everything at Hollow Creek Farms is raised to peak ripeness. We prioritize natural cultivation methods to bring out the deepest, freshest flavors nature has to offer.

Beyond our fields, our Farm Market is stocked with artisanal goods, including raw honey harvested right here on the property, fresh-baked pies, house-made preserves, and hand-pressed apple cider.

Unforgettable Farm Experiences

Creating lasting memories across every season.

Hollow Creek Farms is more than a market—it is a place to connect with the land and celebrate the seasons. Throughout the spring and summer, families gather for our Pick-Your-Own berry events and educational farm tours where kids can meet our friendly farm animals.

As the air turns crisp, the farm transforms for our annual Fall Harvest Festival. Get lost in our intricate corn maze, enjoy a scenic tractor hayride through the orchards, and search through acres of vines to find your absolute perfect pumpkin.

03 / Deeply Rooted

Our Farm History

Passed down across three generations, our farm is a testament to family heritage.

Founded over fifty years ago as a humble, single-acre vegetable patch, Hollow Creek Farms has grown through decades of sweat, passion, and stubborn dedication. What began with grandpa selling sweet corn out of the back of an old farm truck has evolved into a sustainable, modern homestead.

Today, the next generation carries the torch forward, prioritizing earth-friendly agricultural practices while preserving the timeless country traditions that make our community feel like home.
